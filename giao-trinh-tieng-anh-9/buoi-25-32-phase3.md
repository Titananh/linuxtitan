## 📚 PHẦN 1: CỤM ĐỘNG TỪ VỚI LOOK

### 1.1. BẢNG CỤM ĐỘNG TỪ LOOK

| Cụm động từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|-------------|-------|-------|
| **look after** | chăm sóc | She looks after her grandmother. |
| **look for** | tìm kiếm | I'm looking for my keys. |
| **look up** | tra cứu | Look up the word in the dictionary. |
| **look forward to** | mong đợi | I look forward to meeting you. |
| **look back** | nhìn lại (quá khứ) | Looking back, I have no regrets. |
| **look into** | điều tra | The police are looking into the case. |
| **look out** | cẩn thận | Look out! There's a car coming! |
| **look around/round** | nhìn quanh | Let's look around the museum. |
| **look down on** | khinh thường | Don't look down on others. |
| **look up to** | ngưỡng mộ | I look up to my father. |

### 1.2. LƯU Ý ĐẶC BIỆT

```
⚠️ LOOK FORWARD TO + V-ING (không phải TO + V)
✅ I look forward to SEEING you.
❌ I look forward to SEE you.
```

### BÀI TẬP: ĐIỀN CỤM ĐỘNG TỪ (20 CÂU)

```
1. I'm ___ my glasses. Have you seen them? (tìm)
2. She has to ___ her younger brother after school. (chăm sóc)
3. If you don't know this word, ___ it ___ in the dictionary. (tra cứu)
4. I ___ ___ ___ our holiday next month. (mong đợi)
5. He ___ ___ smoking last year. (từ bỏ)
6. The fire ___ ___ a lot of smoke. (tỏa ra)
7. Don't ___ ___! You can win! (đầu hàng)
8. The teacher ___ ___ the exam papers. (phân phát)
9. She ___ ___ her old clothes to charity. (cho đi)
10. ___ ___! There's a hole in the ground! (cẩn thận)
11. The police are ___ ___ the crime. (điều tra)
12. I ___ ___ ___ my parents. (ngưỡng mộ)
13. Don't ___ ___ ___ poor people. (khinh thường)
14. Let's ___ ___ the city before dinner. (nhìn quanh)
15. ___ ___, I realize I made a mistake. (nhìn lại)
16. Please ___ ___ my book. I need it now. (trả lại)
17. The secret was ___ ___ by his friend. (tiết lộ)
18. She never ___ ___ hope. (từ bỏ)
19. We ___ ___ ___ seeing our grandparents. (mong đợi)
20. ___ ___ new words every day. (tra cứu)
```

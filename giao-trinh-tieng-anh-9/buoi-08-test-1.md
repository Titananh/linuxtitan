## PHẦN V: ĐỌC HIỂU (10 điểm)

**Read the passage and answer the questions:**

```
LIFE IN VIETNAM IN THE PAST

Life in Vietnam was very different fifty years ago. People used to live 
in small villages and most families were farmers. There were no cars or 
motorbikes, so people had to walk or ride bicycles to go anywhere.

Children used to help their parents with housework and farming. They 
didn't have smartphones or computers for entertainment. Instead, they 
played traditional games and listened to stories from their grandparents.

Schools were different too. There were fewer facilities, and many 
children, especially girls, couldn't go to school because their families 
were poor. Education was not as accessible as it is today.

Life was harder, but people were closer to each other. Neighbors helped 
each other and communities were stronger. Many older people wish that 
today's generation could experience the simple life of the past.
```

### PHẦN VI: VIẾT (10 điểm)

**Tiêu chí chấm:**
- Nội dung đầy đủ, đúng chủ đề: 4 điểm
- Ngữ pháp chính xác (sử dụng đúng used to, wish): 3 điểm
- Từ vựng phong phú: 2 điểm
- Đủ số từ, trình bày rõ ràng: 1 điểm

**Mẫu bài viết:**

```
When I was younger, I used to live in a small village with my 
grandparents. Life was very simple and peaceful. I used to play 
with my friends in the rice fields every afternoon. We didn't 
have smartphones or computers, so we played traditional games 
like hide and seek.

I used to help my grandmother cook dinner. She used to tell me 
stories about her childhood. Now I live in the city and go to 
school every day. I wish I could visit my grandparents more 
often. I miss the simple life in the countryside.
```

### ✅ Quy trình chấm và chữa bài:
